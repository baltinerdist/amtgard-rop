## Requirements

- **only-enchantments**: only works on Enchantments — E1 speaks of the Enchantment returning, so the next ability must be an Enchantment. *(E1)*

## Clarifications in the text

- The Enchantment keeps returning after each respawn until it is removed some other way. *(E1)*
- Persistent is Meta-Magic: used before another incantation, it affects the next ability cast by the same caster. *(N1)*
- It does not make an Enchantment already on a player Persistent retroactively. *(N2)*
- Persistent Enchantments with limited uses (Mass Healing, Corrosive Mist, etc.) keep the number of uses they had left. *(N3)*
- Persistent Magic Armor behaves like normal armor: repaired on respawn, but keeps prior damage if the bearer returns to life another way (e.g. Resurrect). *(N4)*
