# Special Effects Defined

Special Effects are a common language used to define battlefield events. Special Effects typically trigger when a player or object is hit by a weapon, Magic Ball, or targeted by a completed Verbal. A single event typically only has one relevant Special Effect. For instance a Destruction Arrow is both Armor Destroying and Shield Destroying, but there is no situation in which both of those matter simultaneously. Likewise a sword may be both Armor Breaking and Wounds Kill, but players are not affected by Armor Breaking and armor is not affected by Wounds Kill.

Special Effects granted to weapons and arrows are always Extraordinary. Special Effects have no associated School, except for Wounds Kill, which is always of the Death School.

### Armor Breaking

An armor location affected by the Armor Breaking Special Effect will reduce the Armor Points of that location to zero if it currently has three or less Armor Points. Otherwise, the armor location is affected as per a normal hit.

### Armor Destroying

An armor location affected by the Armor Destroying Special Effect reduces the Armor Points to zero in that location.

### Phasing

Hits from equipment with the Phasing Special Effect, and any other Special Effect delivered by it, do not interact with ongoing abilities nor Traits. For example:

1. An Imbued Shield hit three times with Shield Crushing from a Phasing melee weapon would be destroyed.
2. The Magic Armor granted by Stoneskin would be ignored.
3. A player enchanted with Phoenix Tears would remain dead after being killed by a Phasing weapon.
4. A Paladin would die from being wounded by a weapon with both the Wounds Kill and Phasing Special Effects.

Phasing hits do not bypass States. For example, a Frozen player would not be affected.

### Shield Crushing

A shield affected by the Shield Crushing Special Effect is damaged. Three cumulative instances of damage from any source will destroy the shield. A shield that has been damaged any amount may be repaired.

### Siege

A strike to a player or their carried equipment by an object with the Siege Special Effect will kill the player and destroy all their carried equipment.

### Shield Destroying

A shield affected by the Shield Destroying Special Effect is destroyed.

### Weapon Destroying

A weapon affected by the Weapon Destroying Special Effect is destroyed.

### Wounds Kill

A player wounded by an object with the Wounds Kill Special Effect dies. Wounds Kill is always of the Death School.

## Declarations

The battlefield is a hectic place, with lots of information flowing back and forth. In order to ease communication, there is certain information that must be shared with other players, called Declarations. States and game mechanics do not prevent you from making Declarations, even if you would otherwise be prevented from speaking. Declarations also do not interrupt Chants or Incantations and may be made while moving. Declarations must be audible out to 20' and to any relevant player(s). Declarations fall into three categories:

### Upon Engagement

This information should be readily available to anyone who is engaging you in active combat, such as aiming a bow at you or squaring up to swing at you. You must share this information as soon as possible and must repeat it if asked.

### Upon Request

This information is not necessary to share proactively, though you may choose to do so if you wish. However, if another player asks you for this information, you must share with them. Similarly, some mechanics require you to make a declaration at specific times.

### Upon Interaction

This information must be shared when it is relevant to resolving the situation at hand. If this information is something you can share ahead of time to smooth gameplay, it is up to you whether to do so.

You are always allowed to communicate information for safety reasons. The same goes for if a Reeve asks for a piece of information such as your spell list. Anything that doesn't fall into the above categories is not required to be shared with other players (and indeed cannot be, if you are not allowed to speak).

> **Made Easy**
>
> **Declarations Made Easy**
>
> **Upon Engagement**
>
> - Dead or Invulnerable
> - Posting or Hobbling
> - Special Effects on melee attacks
> - Enchantments or a summary of their effects
>   - Whether the enchantment is Persistent
>   - Choices made at the time of casting, such as weapons or shields for Harden
>   - You are not required to share the number of uses remaining on multi-use enchantments
> - Presence of Magic Armor
>
> **Upon Request**
>
> - Your name, class, and which team you are on
>   - Which Monster you're playing or Archetype you have purchased
> - Your current wounds, States, and ongoing effects
> - If your weapon or shield is broken, heated, or otherwise unable to be wielded
> - The presence of remaining armor points in a location
>   - You are not required to share the specific number of points remaining unless it is zero
> - Whether you are a willing target for an ability
>
> **Upon Interacton**
>
> - Calling dead or alive
> - Calling shots upon yourself, including:
>   - hand on weapon
>   - foot on ground
>   - that a shot only struck garb
> - That you are Immune or otherwise unaffected by a spell
>   - When dead, this includes whether you are Cursed and if you have moved more than 5' from where you died
