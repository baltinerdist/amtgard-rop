# Dervish

**Available to:** Bard 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Equipment costs are doubled. Each Verbal purchased gives double the uses. Example: 1/Life Charge x3 becomes 2/Life Charge x3, 2/Life becomes 4/Life, 1/Refresh becomes 2/Refresh.
