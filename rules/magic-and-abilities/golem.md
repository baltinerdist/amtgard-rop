# Golem

**Available to:** Druid 4

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "From earth and clay I form thee" x3

**Materials:** White strip and yellow strip

**Effect:** Bearer is Immune to Death. Bearer is Cursed. Bearer can remove a wound via Mend. Bearer may use the caster as an alternate respawn point while the caster is alive. Bearer may treat the caster as an Alternate Base. All Enchantments worn by the Bearer, including Golem, are Persistent while Golem is worn. Golem remains active while the bearer is dead.

**Limitations:** A caster may only have a single Golem Enchantment at a time and may not use Alternate Bases. Bearer may not be treated as an Alternate Base.

**Note:** Greater Mend and Word of Mending will not remove a wound.
