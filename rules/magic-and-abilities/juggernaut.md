# Juggernaut

**Available to:** Warrior 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Harden Armor (Self) 1/Life (ex) and Phoenix Tears (Self) 3/Refresh (ex) (Swift). Replace Harden with Greater Harden (Self) (ex) at the same frequency.

**Limitations:** Lose all instances of Ancestral Armor and True Grit.
