# Greater Heal

**Available to:** Healer 4, Paladin 2

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Spirit

**Range:** Touch

**Incantation:** "By the grace of the divine thou art healed" x5

**Effect:** All wounds are healed. Ignores the Cursed State.
