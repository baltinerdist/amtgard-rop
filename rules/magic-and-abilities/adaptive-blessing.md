# Adaptive Blessing

**Available to:** Healer 2

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I enchant thee with this blessing" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Bearer becomes Resistant to one of the following Schools: Death, Flame, Subdual, Command, Sorcery. School is chosen at the time of casting. Does not count towards the bearer's Enchantment limit.

**Limitations:** May not be worn with any other Enchantments from the Protection School unless the other Enchantment is (ex).
