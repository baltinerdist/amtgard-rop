# Ironskin

**Available to:** Druid 5

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I enchant thee with Ironskin" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Bearer is Immune to Flame and gains two points Magic Armor affected as per Ancestral Armor.
