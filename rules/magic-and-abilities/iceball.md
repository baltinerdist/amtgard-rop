# Iceball

**Available to:** Druid 4, Healer 3, Wizard 3

**Type:** Magic Ball

**School:** Subdual

**Incantation:** "The strength of ice is mine to evoke" x3

**Materials:** White Magic Ball

**Effect:** Target player becomes Frozen for 60 seconds. Engulfing.
