# Swift

**Available to:** Monk 6, Bard 4, Druid 4, Healer 4, Wizard 4

**Type:** Meta-Magic

**School:** Neutral

**Incantation:** "Swift"

**Effect:** Abilities require only a single iteration of the incantation. For multi-line Incantations use the last line.

**Limitations:** May only be used on abilities at a range of Touch, Other, Self, or on Magic Balls. May not be used on the Charge Incantation.
