# Harden Armor

**Available to:** Warrior 6, Druid 1

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Self (Wa), Other (Dr)

**Incantation:** "I enchant thee with harden armor" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Armor Breaking strikes to the bearer's armor are treated as regular strikes.

**Limitations:** Does not apply to Magic Armor.
