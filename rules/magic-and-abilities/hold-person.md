# Hold Person

**Available to:** Assassin 4, Healer 2, Scout 4, Wizard 3

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Command

**Range:** 20'

**Incantation:** "I command thee to stop" x3

**Effect:** Target player becomes Stopped for 30 seconds.
