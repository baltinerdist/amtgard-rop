# Scavenge

**Available to:** Warrior 2

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Self

**Incantation:** "Scavenge"

**Effect:** A destroyed or damaged item carried by the caster is repaired, or one point of armor in one of the caster's hit locations is repaired.

**Limitations:** Kill Trigger.
