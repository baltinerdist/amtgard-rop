# Song of Deflection

**Available to:** Bard 4

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Self

**Incantation:** "I sing of my nimble acrobatics"

**Effect:** Bearer is unaffected by projectiles other than Magic Balls. Engulfing effects from those projectiles, such as Pinning Arrow, do not affect the bearer. Bearer must Chant "Song of Deflection" or sing a song of their acrobatic prowess. Singing in place of the normal Chant is still a Chant and must follow all Chant rules.

**Note:** Equipment can still be affected by the above.
