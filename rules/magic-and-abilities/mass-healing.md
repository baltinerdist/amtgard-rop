# Mass Healing

**Available to:** Healer 6

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Spirit

**Range:** Self

**Incantation:** "Let the powers of healing flow through me" x3

**Materials:** Five yellow strips

**Effect:** Caster may Heal (m) a player at Touch by declaring "I grant thee healing" and removing an enchantment strip. Enchantment is removed when the last strip is removed.

**Note:** The declaration is not an incantation, and so is not stopped by being Suppressed, and may be used while moving, etc.
