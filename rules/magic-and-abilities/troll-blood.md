# Troll Blood

**Available to:** Druid 5

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "The blood of the trolls sustains thee" x3

**Materials:** Three white strips

**Effect:** Bearer does not die as normal. When the bearer would otherwise die they instead ignore the triggering effect as though it had not occurred, remove a strip, and become Frozen for 30 seconds. The bearer is treated as though they have the effects of Regeneration in addition to the above.

**Note:** Troll Blood is removed when the last strip is removed.
