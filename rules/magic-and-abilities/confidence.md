# Confidence

**Available to:** Bard 1

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "My power grants thee confidence"

**Effect:** Target player may instantly Charge a single ability.

**Limitations:** May not be used within 20' of a living enemy.
