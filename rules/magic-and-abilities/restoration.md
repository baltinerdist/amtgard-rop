# Restoration

**Available to:** Bard 4

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I restore thee to thy full potency"

**Effect:** Player has all uses of their per-life abilities restored.

**Limitations:** Does not function on Empower, Confidence, or Restoration.
