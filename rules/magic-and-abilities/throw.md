# Throw

**Available to:** Wizard 3

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** 20'

**Incantation:** "My power throws thee" x3

**Effect:** Target player is moved back 50' in a straight line away from the caster. Works on Stopped and Stunned players. If the caster is the target, the caster may choose the direction they move.

**Note:** This is a Forced Movement effect. This effect is ended if the target is affected by another Forced Movement effect or becomes Frozen, Insubstantial, Invulnerable, or Stunned.
