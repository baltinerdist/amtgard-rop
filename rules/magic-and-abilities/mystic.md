# Mystic

**Available to:** Monk 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Force Bolt 3 Balls / Unlimited (m), Phase Bolt 1 Ball / Unlimited (m), and Suppression Bolt 2 Balls / Unlimited (m).

**Limitations:** May not wield Heavy Thrown. Lose all instances of Resurrect.
