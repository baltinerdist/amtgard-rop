# Mystic

**Available to:** Monk 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Force Bolt 4 Balls / Unlimited (m) and Suppression Bolt 2 Balls / Unlimited (m).

**Limitations:** May not wield Heavy Thrown. Lose all instances of Resurrect.
