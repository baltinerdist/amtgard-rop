# Icy Blast

**Available to:** Druid 3, Wizard 4

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** 20'

**Incantation:** "My power makes thee frozen" x3

**Effect:** Target player becomes Frozen for 30 seconds.
