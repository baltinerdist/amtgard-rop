# Contagion

**Available to:** Wizard 5

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Death

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "May thou bear this plague to all" x3

**Materials:** Red strip

**Effect:** Bearers wielded melee weapons are Wounds Kill. Bearer is Fragile.
