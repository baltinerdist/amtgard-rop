# Marauder

**Available to:** Warrior 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Momentum Unlimited (ex) (Ambulant). Insult becomes 1/Life Charge x5 (m) (Ambulant).

**Limitations:** Maximum Armor becomes 4pts. May not wield Large shields. Ancestral Armor is no longer chargeable.
