# Berserker

**Available to:** Barbarian 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Momentum Unlimited (ex) (Ambulant).

**Limitations:** May not wear Armor, and lose all instances of Blood and Thunder.
