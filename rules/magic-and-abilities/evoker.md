# Evoker

**Available to:** Wizard 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Elemental Barrage becomes Charge x10.

**Limitations:** May not purchase Verbals with a range of 20' or 50'.

**Note:** Elemental Barrage must still be purchased.
