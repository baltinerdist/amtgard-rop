# Gift of Earth

**Available to:** Druid 2

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I grant thee a gift of the earth" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Bearer gains one point of Magic Armor and is affected as per Harden.
