# Gift of Water

**Available to:** Druid 4

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I grant thee a gift of the water" x3

**Materials:** White strip and yellow strip

**Effect:** Bearer gains one point of Magic Armor and Heal (Self) Unlimited (m).
