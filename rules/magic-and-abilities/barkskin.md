# Barkskin

**Available to:** Druid 1

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I enchant thee with barkskin" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Bearer gains one point of Magic Armor.
