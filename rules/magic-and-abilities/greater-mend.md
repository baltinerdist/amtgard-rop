# Greater Mend

**Available to:** Archer 6, Druid 3, Wizard 3

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Touch

**Incantation:** "Return this `<object name>` to its former glory" x5

**Effect:** Will restore all armor points in one location or repair a damaged or broken item.
