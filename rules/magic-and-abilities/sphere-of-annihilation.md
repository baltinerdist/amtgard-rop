# Sphere of Annihilation

**Available to:** Wizard 6

**Type:** Magic Ball

**School:** Sorcery

**Incantation:** "The power of void is mine to evoke" x3

**Materials:** Black Magic Ball

**Effect:** This Magic Ball ignores armor and Enchantments. Additionally, will have one of the following effects: 1. A weapon hit is destroyed 2. A shield hit is subject to Shield Destroying. 3. A player hit dies and is Cursed.

**Note:** Because Sphere of Annihilation ignores Enchantments, A player enchanted with Phoenix Tears would remain dead after being killed, and an Imbued Shield would still be destroyed.
