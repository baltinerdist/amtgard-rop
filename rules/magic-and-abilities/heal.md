# Heal

**Available to:** Druid 2, Healer 1, Monk 1, Scout 1

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Spirit

**Range:** Touch

**Incantation:** "The white light of healing hath healed thee." x5

**Effect:** Target player heals a wound.
