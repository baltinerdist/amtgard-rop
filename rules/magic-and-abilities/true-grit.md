# True Grit

**Available to:** Warrior 3

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Spirit

**Range:** Self

**Incantation:** "The wicked flee when I pursue" immediately after dying

**Effect:** Caster returns to life with their wounds healed and is immediately Frozen for 30 seconds.

**Note:** Enchantments on the caster are retained.
