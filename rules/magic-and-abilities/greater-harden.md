# Greater Harden

**Available to:** Warrior 6, Healer 3

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I enchant thee with Greater Harden" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Shields and weapons wielded by the player are affected as per Harden.
