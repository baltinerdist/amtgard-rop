# Gift of Fire

**Available to:** Druid 3

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Flame

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I grant thee a gift of the fire" x3

**Materials:** Red strip and white strip

**Effect:** Bearer gains Heat Weapon 1/Refresh Charge x3 (m) and is Immune to Flame.
