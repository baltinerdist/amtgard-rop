# Guardian

**Available to:** Paladin 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Imbue (Touch) 1/Life (m) and Martyr (Other) 2/Life Charge x3 (ex).

**Limitations:** Lose all instances of Protection from Evil and Protection from Magic. May only have one instance of Imbue active at a time.
