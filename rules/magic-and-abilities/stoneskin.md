# Stoneskin

**Available to:** Druid 3

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "May nature protect thee from all forms of attack" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Bearer gains 2 points of Magic Armor affected as per Ancestral Armor.
