# Missile Block

**Available to:** Monk 1

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Self

**Effect:** Bearer may block projectiles, including Magic Balls, with their hands and with wielded melee or thrown weapons. Any projectile touched in this way is nullified.
