# Missile Block

**Available to:** Monk 1

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Self

**Effect:** Player may block arrows, projectile weapons, and Magic Balls with their hands and wielded melee or thrown weapons. Any arrow, projectile weapon or magic ball blocked is nullified.
