# Suppression Bolt

**Available to:** Monk 6, Wizard 2

**Type:** Magic Ball

**School:** Subdual

**Incantation:** "The strength of suppression is mine to evoke" x3

**Materials:** Purple Magic Ball

**Effect:** Target is Suppressed for 60 seconds. Engulfing.
