# Force Bolt

**Available to:** Monk 6, Druid 2, Wizard 1

**Type:** Magic Ball

**School:** Sorcery

**Incantation:** "Forcebolt" x3

**Materials:** Blue Magic Ball

**Effect:** This Magic Ball is Weapon Destroying and Armor Breaking. Player hit receives a wound to that hit location.
