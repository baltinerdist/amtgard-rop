# Empower

**Available to:** Bard 2

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I empower thee"

**Effect:** Target player regains one use of any per-life ability they have expended.

**Limitations:** Does not function on Empower, Confidence, or Restoration.

**Note:** Does not allow a player to have more than their maximum uses of an ability.
