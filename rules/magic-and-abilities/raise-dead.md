# Raise Dead

**Available to:** Healer 3

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Death

**Range:** Touch

**Incantation:** "Rise and fight again" x5

**Effect:** Target willing dead player who has not moved more than 5' from where they died is returned to life and is Cursed. Target is also Suppressed for 30 seconds. Non-Persistent Enchantments on the player are removed before the player returns to life. Any wounds on the player are healed.
