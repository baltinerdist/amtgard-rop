# Enlightened Soul

**Available to:** Healer 5, Monk 1

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other (He) Self (Mk)

**Incantation:** "A distant magic has no hold upon thy now enlightened soul" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Bearer is unaffected by Verbal Magical abilities used at a Range greater than Touch.

**Limitations:** Affects beneficial as well as harmful Magical abilities.

**Note:** Does not affect (ex) abilities, abilities with a Range of Touch, nor abilities whose Range is greater than Touch but are used at a Range of Touch anyway.
