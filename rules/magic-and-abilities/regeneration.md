# Regeneration

**Available to:** Druid 3

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Spirit

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I grant thee the power of regeneration" x3

**Materials:** Yellow strip

**Effect:** Bearer gains Heal (Self) Unlimited (m) (Swift).

**Limitations:** The Heal granted by Regeneration may not be used within 10' of a living enemy.

**Note:** Bearer must state Swift normally.
