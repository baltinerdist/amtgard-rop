# Shake It Off

**Available to:** Warrior 5

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Spirit

**Range:** Self

**Incantation:** "I shall overcome"

**Effect:** 10 seconds after casting Shake It Off the caster may remove from themselves any number of States or Ongoing Effects of their choice. Shake It Off may be cast at any time the caster is alive, even while the caster would otherwise be prevented from casting abilities by Stunned, Suppressed, or similar.

**Note:** Immunity to Shake It Off only matters when the ability is cast, not after 10 seconds.
