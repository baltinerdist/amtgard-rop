# Apex

**Available to:** Scout 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Mend 1/Life (ex) and Sleight of Mind (Self) 1/Life (ex).

**Limitations:** Lose all instances of Evolution, Hold Person, and Pinning Arrow.
