# Corruptor

**Available to:** Anti-Paladin 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** Gain Void Touched (Self) 2/Refresh (m). All uses of Terror become 1/Life Charge x10 (m).

**Limitations:** May not wield Great Weapons or Javelins and lose all instances of Flame Blade.
