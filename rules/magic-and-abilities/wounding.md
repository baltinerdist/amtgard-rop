# Wounding

**Available to:** Wizard 4

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Death

**Range:** 20'

**Incantation:** "Death strikes off thy [left/right] [arm/leg]" x3

**Effect:** Target hit location on target player is wounded.

**Limitations:** Has no effect on players already wounded.
