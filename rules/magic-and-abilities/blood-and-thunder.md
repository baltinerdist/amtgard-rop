# Blood and Thunder

**Available to:** Barbarian 6

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Spirit

**Range:** Self

**Incantation:** "Blood and Thunder!"

**Effect:** Caster gains Blessing Against Wounds (ex).

**Limitations:** Kill Trigger.

**Note:** Caster must still wear a white strip to denote Blessing Against Wounds.
