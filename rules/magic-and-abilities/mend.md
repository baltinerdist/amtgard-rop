# Mend

**Available to:** Archer 2, Bard 2, Druid 1, Healer 3, Wizard 1

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Touch

**Incantation:** "I make this item whole again" x5

**Effect:** Destroyed or damaged item is repaired, or one point of armor in one location is repaired.
