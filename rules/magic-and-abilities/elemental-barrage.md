# Elemental Barrage

**Available to:** Wizard 6

**Type:** Verbal

**School:** Sorcery

**Range:** Self

**Incantation:** "I am filled with the power of magic"

**Effect:** Caster may use Magic Balls they are currently carrying by declaring the name of the Magic Ball immediately prior to throwing the ball in place of the incantation.

**Limitations:** This magic ends if the caster picks up any additional Magic Balls or begins casting any new Magical abilities.

**Note:** The declaration is not an incantation, and so is not stopped by being Suppressed, and may be used while moving, etc.
