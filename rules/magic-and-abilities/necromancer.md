# Necromancer

**Available to:** Healer 6

**Type:** Archetype

**School:** Neutral

**Effect:** All abilities purchased in the Death School become Charge x3. Bearer may have a combined total of five Undead Minion Enchantments.

**Limitations:** Bearer may not purchase any abilities from the Protection School.
