# Gift of Air

**Available to:** Druid 5

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I grant thee a gift of the air" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** The effects of any weapon or ammunition which just struck the bearer are ignored, instead the bearer declares "Gift of Air" and becomes Insubstantial. If the bearer is wearing armor it is affected as normal in addition to triggering Gift of Air. Bearer may choose to return directly to their base immediately after Gift of Air activates as a Forced Movement effect. Melee weapons with the Siege, Armor Breaking, Armor Destroying, Shield Crushing, or Shield Destroying Special Effects will affect the bearer as normal and do not trigger Gift of Air.

**Limitations:** Bearer may not wield weapons or Shields.

**Note:** If the Insubstantial State is ended, the player is not required to continue returning to base. Bearer may end this Insubstantial State at any time by using the exit incantation for Insubstantial.
