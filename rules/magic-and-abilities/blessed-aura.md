# Blessed Aura

**Available to:** Healer 5

**Type:** Enchantment

**School:** Protection

**Range:** Other

**Incantation:** "I enchant thy person, arms, and armor" x3

**Materials:** White strip

**Effect:** Bearer is resistant to all effects from the next source which would inflict a wound, death, State, or negatively affect them or their carried equipment. Does not trigger against effects cast by the bearer.
