# Anti-Paladin

The Anti-Paladin class is designed for aggressive front-line combat, with a focus on offense and disrupting enemies.

- **Examples:** Mordred, the Black Knight, Sir Francis Dashwood, Xolotl
- **Garb:** Metallic silver sash and medieval/sword and sorcery-looking garb. Knights may wear a white belt and a black phoenix Class Symbol instead.
- **Requirements:** Must be 6th level in at least one class.
- **Look The Part:** Terror 1/Life (m)

## Equipment

- **Armor:** 4pts
- **Shields:** Large
- **Weapons:** All Melee, Javelins

## Level Progression

## Abilities

### Terror

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Death
**Range:** 20'
**Incantation:** "Death makes thee terrified" x3
**Effect:** Target may not attack or cast Magical abilities at the caster or their carried equipment. Target must remain at least 50' away from the caster unless forced there by another Forced Movement effect. Lasts 30 seconds.
**Note:** If the caster attacks the target, begins casting another Magical ability at the target or their carried equipment, or dies, this ability's effect is negated. This is a Forced Movement effect.

### Poison

**Type:** Enchantment
**School:** Death
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "I coat these weapons with a deadly poison" x2
**Materials:** Red strip
**Effect:** The next wound dealt by the bearer with a wielded melee weapon is Wounds Kill.
**Note:** If the target does not actually receive a wound, e.g. by a Resistance, Poison is not expended.

### Steal Life Essence

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Death
**Range:** Touch
**Incantation:** "Steal life"
**Effect:** Target dead player is Cursed. Caster may heal a wound or instantly Charge an ability.
**Limitations:** Does not work on Cursed players.
**Note:** Caster will always benefit if successfully cast on a valid target, regardless of the caster's Traits, States, Immunities, Ongoing Effects, or Enchantments. In order to charge an ability, the name of the ability being charged must still be stated immediately after the incantation.

### Brutal Strike

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Death
**Range:** Unlimited
**Incantation:** "And stay down!"
**Effect:** Target player is Cursed indefinitely. Target player is also Suppressed for 30 seconds.
**Limitations:** Wound Trigger.
**Note:** Brutal Strike targets the wounded or dead player and does not require verbal targeting.

### Flame Blade

**Type:** Enchantment
**School:** Flame
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "The element of fire shall infuse thy weapons" x3
**Materials:** Red strip and white strip
**Effect:** Bearer's wielded melee weapons are Armor Breaking and Shield Crushing. Bearer and their wielded weapons are Immune to Flame.

### Infernal

**Type:** Archetype
**School:** Neutral
**Effect:** Gain Fireball 2 Balls / Unlimited (m). Flame Blade becomes (Self) 2/Refresh Charge x5.
**Limitations:** May not wield shields and lose all instances of Steal Life Essence.

### Fireball

**Type:** Magic Ball
**School:** Flame
**Incantation:** "The flame of fire is mine to evoke" x3
**Materials:** Red Magic Ball
**Effect:** This Magic Ball is Weapon Destroying, Armor Destroying, and Shield Destroying. Player hit dies.

### Corruptor

**Type:** Archetype
**School:** Neutral
**Effect:** Gain Void Touched (Self) 2/Refresh (m). All uses of Terror become 1/Life Charge x10 (m).
**Limitations:** May not wield Great Weapons or Javelins and lose all instances of Flame Blade.

### Void Touched

**Type:** Enchantment
**School:** Sorcery
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "Embrace the old ones and surrender thyself" x3
**Materials:** Red strip and white strip
**Effect:** Bearer's wielded melee weapons are Armor Breaking. Bearer gains Shadow Step 1/Refresh Charge x30 (ex), Steal Life Essence Unlimited (ex), and is unaffected by Magical abilities from the Sorcery, Spirit, and Death Schools. Bearer is Cursed.
**Note:** This effect does not interact with other Enchantments worn by the bearer.

### Shadow Step

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Sorcery
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "I step into the shadows"
**Effect:** Caster becomes Insubstantial. Shadow Step may be cast while moving.
**Note:** Caster may end this Insubstantial State at any time by using the exit incantation for Insubstantial.
