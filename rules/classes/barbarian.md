# Barbarian

The Barbarian class is designed for aggressive front-line fighting, with a focus on melee fighting and endurance.

**Examples:** Leif Eriksson, Attila the Hun, Alaric the Vandal

**Garb:** White sash and medieval/sword and sorcery looking garb

**Look The Part:** Rage 1/Refresh Charge x10 (ex) (Ambulant)

## Equipment

- **Armor:** 3pts
- **Shields:** Medium
- **Weapons:** All Melee, Javelins, Rocks

## Level Progression

### Barbarian Abilities By Level

| Level | Abilities |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | Berserk (T); Immune to Command (T); Immune to Subdual (T) |
| 2nd | Rage 1/Refresh Charge x10 (ex) (Ambulant) |
| 3rd | Adrenaline - Unlimited (ex) |
| 4th | Rage 1/Refresh Charge x10 (ex) (Ambulant) |
| 5th | Brutal Strike 1/Life Charge x3 (ex) (Ambulant) |
| 6th | Blood and Thunder - Unlimited (ex); Optional – Pick one: Raider (A); Berserker (A) |

## Abilities

### Berserk

**Type:** Granted as a Trait
**School:** Sorcery
**Range:** Self
**Effect:** Bearer's wielded melee weapons are Armor Breaking.

### Rage

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Sorcery
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "I am filled with rage!"
**Effect:** Caster is unaffected by Verbal abilities and their wielded melee weapons are Shield Crushing and Armor Breaking for seven seconds. Caster must count this time out loud, audible to 50'. Failure to count or starting an Incantation ends the effect.

### Adrenaline

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Spirit
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "Adrenaline"
**Effect:** Caster heals a wound.
**Limitations:** Kill Trigger.

### Brutal Strike

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Death
**Range:** Unlimited
**Incantation:** "And stay down!"
**Effect:** Target player is Cursed indefinitely. Target player is also Suppressed for 30 seconds.
**Limitations:** Wound Trigger.
**Note:** Brutal Strike targets the wounded or dead player and does not require verbal targeting.

### Blood and Thunder

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Spirit
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "Blood and Thunder!"
**Effect:** Caster gains Blessing Against Wounds (ex).
**Limitations:** Kill Trigger.
**Note:** Caster must still wear a white strip to denote Blessing Against Wounds.

### Blessing Against Wounds

**Type:** Enchantment
**School:** Protection
**Range:** Self
**Materials:** White strip
**Effect:** Bearer is resistant to wounds. Does not count towards the bearer's Enchantment limit.
**Limitations:** May not be worn with any other Enchantments from the Protection School unless the other Enchantment is (ex).

### Raider

**Type:** Archetype
**School:** Neutral
**Effect:** Gain Bear Strength (Self) 1/Life (ex). Look the Part becomes an additional use of Brutal Strike.
**Limitations:** Lose all instances of Rage.

### Bear Strength

**Type:** Enchantment
**School:** Sorcery
**Range:** Other
**Incantation:** "I enchant thee with the strength of the bear" x3
**Materials:** Red strip
**Effect:** Bearer's wielded melee weapons are Shield Crushing.

### Berserker

**Type:** Archetype
**School:** Neutral
**Effect:** Gain Momentum Unlimited (ex) (Ambulant).
**Limitations:** May not wear Armor, and lose all instances of Blood and Thunder.

### Momentum

**Type:** Verbal
**School:** Sorcery
**Range:** Self
**Incantation:** "Momentum"
**Effect:** May be used to instantly Charge a single ability by stating its name.
**Limitations:** Kill Trigger
