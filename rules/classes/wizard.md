# Wizard

The Wizard class is designed for powerful ranged offense and battlefield control, with a focus on damaging and disrupting enemies.

**Examples:** Morgana le Fey, Merlin, The Wicked Witch of the West, Carman, Abe no Seimei

**Garb:** Yellow sash and medieval/sword and sorcery looking garb

**Look The Part:** 1 extra magic point at the Wizard's highest level.

## Equipment

- **Armor:** None
- **Shields:** None
- **Weapons:** Dagger, Magic Staff

## Magic User

Wizards may purchase five magic points from each level. Unused points from higher levels can be rolled down to lower levels. A list of all magic purchased must be carried at all times. All abilities purchased by Magic Users are Magical abilities.
