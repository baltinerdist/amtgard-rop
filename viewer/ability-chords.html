How this is worked out. Every class is treated as 6th level with every ability on its list, spells included.
  • Archetypes, spell points and purchase limits are ignored, and the Equipment traits are left out.
  • A green or red line starts at an ability that can affect another player (a Verbal or Enchantment with a range of Other, Touch, 20′, 50′ or Unlimited, or a Magic Ball or Specialty Arrow). Self-only abilities and Traits get no green or red lines, so they show as hollow dots.
  • Red means the player cannot be affected. That comes from class Immunities to a School (Anti-Paladin, Barbarian, Paladin) and from the Monk’s Enlightened Soul, which stops Verbal Magical abilities used beyond Touch (a Monk is still affected at Touch, and (ex) abilities always affect a Monk). Martial abilities count as Magical only when the class table marks them (m).
  • Enchantments ignore Immunities (Enchantments rule 3), so Poison, Flame Blade and similar show green on immune classes. Abilities they grant do not: Undead Minion on a Paladin, and Poison Arrow on a Paladin, are red because part of the effect is stopped (see the tooltip).
  • Immunities and Enlightened Soul protect the player, not their equipment. Pyrotechnics, Destroy Armor and Shatter Weapon therefore show green on immune classes, Heat Weapon is green on a Monk, and Fireball and Lightning Bolt are red on an Anti-Paladin even though their equipment is still hit.
  • Touch and Other range abilities also need a willing, dead, stunned, frozen or Insubstantial target, and temporary defenses such as Rage or Sanctuary are not counted. Both apply to every class alike.
  • Blue joins the same ability when it appears on more than one class.
  • Condensed merges all the green lines between two classes into one ribbon whose width shows how many abilities it carries. Red and blue lines stay one per ability, and choosing Every ability draws them all.
