Anti-PaladinMartial · Lvl 1 Aggressive front-line offense & disruption.⚑ Must be 6th level in another class
Armor 4 pts  ·  Shields Large  ·  Weapons All Melee, Javelins  ·  Garb Metallic silver sash  ·  Look The Part Terror 1/Life (m)
Traits: Immune to Command, Immune to Flame. Look The Part: Terror.
Immune to Command Trait · Command
Always on: unaffected by abilities from the Command School. Cannot be removed; needs no incantation.
Immune to Flame Trait · Flame
Always on: unaffected by abilities from the Flame School. Cannot be removed; needs no incantation.
Terror LTP · 1/Life · (m) · Death · 20'
“Death makes thee terrified" x3” Target may not attack or cast Magical abilities at the caster or their carried equipment. Target must remain at least 50' away from the caster unless forced there by another Forced Movement effect. Lasts 30 seconds. Note: If the caster attacks the target, begins casting another Magical ability at the target or their carried equipment, or dies, this ability's effect is negated. This is a Forced Movement effect.
ArcherMartial · Lvl 1 Ranged combat with enhanced arrows.
Armor 2 pts  ·  Shields None  ·  Weapons Dagger, Short, Bow  ·  Garb Orange sash  ·  Look The Part Pick one arrow (1 Arrow/Unlim, ex)
Get: Reload + pick two of three arrows. Look The Part: pick one arrow.
Reload 1/Refresh Charge x3 · (ex) · Sorcery · Self
“I nocked my arrows to my bow, I let them fly, my quiver is low. Now I pause to go reload." x3” Player becomes Invulnerable and may move about the field retrieving their arrows. The player may remove their Invulnerable State in the location they started or at base by stating, "I return with a full quiver" x3. Limits: Must stay at least 10' away from other players at all times. A player may not exit Reload at an alternate base location, such as Heart of the Swarm, in this way. Note: May ask reeve for assistance in retrieving arrows that are within 10' of other players.
Destruction Arrow 1 Arrow/Unlim · (ex) · Sorcery
“Destruction Arrow” This arrow is Armor Destroying and Shield Destroying. Armor Destroying and Shield Destroying are applied after the normal effect of being hit with an arrow is applied.
Pinning Arrow 1 Arrow/Unlim · (ex) · Sorcery
“Pinning Arrow” A player struck by this arrow is Stopped for 30 seconds. Engulfing.
Poison Arrow 1 Arrow/Unlim · (ex) · Death
“Poison Arrow” This arrow is Wounds Kill.

Trickery Trait · Sorcery · Self
Bearer may cast Blink, Shadow Step, and Teleport on themselves while they are already Insubstantial, provided that they were the cause of the initial Insubstantial State and entered it voluntarily. Doing so removes the original Insubstantial effect.
Assassinate Unlimited · (ex) · Ambulant · Death · 50'
“Assassinate” The target is Cursed. Limits: May only be used immediately upon killing an enemy. Note: Assassinate targets the killed enemy and does not require verbal targeting.
Shadow Step 2/Life · (ex) · Sorcery · Self
“I step into the shadows” Player becomes Insubstantial. Shadow Step may be cast while moving. Note: Caster may end this Insubstantial State at any time by using the exit incantation for Insubstantial.
Poison LTP option · 1/Life Charge x3 · (ex) · Death · Self
“I coat these weapons with a deadly poison" x2” The next wound dealt by the bearer with a wielded melee weapon is Wounds Kill. Note: If the target does not actually receive a wound, e.g. by a Resistance, Poison is not expended.
Poison Arrow LTP option · 1 Arrow/Unlim · (ex) · Death
“Poison Arrow” This arrow is Wounds Kill.

Enlightened Soul Trait · Protection · Other
“A distant magic has no hold upon thy now enlightened soul" x3” Player is unaffected by Verbal Magical abilities used at a Range greater than Touch. Limits: Affects beneficial as well as harmful Magical abilities. Note: Does not affect (ex) abilities, abilities with a Range of Touch, nor abilities whose Range is greater than Touch but are used at a Range of Touch anyway.
Missile Block Trait · Protection · Self
Player may block arrows, projectile weapons, and Magic Balls with their hands and wielded melee or thrown weapons. Any arrow, projectile weapon or magic ball blocked is nullified.

Harden 1/Life · (ex) · Protection · Other
“I enchant thee with Harden" x3” Bearer's wielded weapons or shield may only be destroyed or damaged by Magic Balls/Verbals which destroy objects e.g. Fireball or Pyrotechnics. Limits: Will only affect either the weapons or the shield of the bearer, not both.
Insult LTP · 1/Life · (m) · Ambulant · Command · 20'
“I command thy attention" x3” Target is unable to attack or cast Magical abilities at anyone other than the caster or their carried equipment for 30 seconds, or until either party dies. If the target of Insult is attacked or has Magical abilities cast on them or their carried equipment by someone other than the caster, the target of Insult becomes able to choose to attack the offending party as well. Note: The target may still charge and throw Magic Balls at the caster.
