# Monk — Interoperability

[Class rules](../../rules/classes/monk.md) · Martial · 7 abilities on its list, 3 of which can affect another player.

## Defenses

- Enlightened Soul: unaffected by Verbal Magical abilities used at a Range greater than Touch. It does not affect (ex) abilities, abilities used at Touch, or carried equipment and worn armor.
- Missile Block can nullify arrows, projectile weapons and Magic Balls with hands or wielded weapons; it needs an active block, so this model still shows them as working.

Archetypes (ignored in this model): Medium, Mystic.

Look The Part (available from 1st level): [Heal](../abilities/heal.md).
